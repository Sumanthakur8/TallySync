How invoices are read

Any supplier: the tool finds invoice no., date, GSTIN, every item line, GST and totals from whatever layout the PDF uses.   UltraTech Cement: recognised instantly with its saved layout (MT × 20 = Bags).   Other suppliers: quantities and units are taken from the invoice itself.   Check: items must equal Taxable Value, and Taxable + GST + freight/TCS + round off must equal the invoice total, otherwise the row is marked Review.   Edit: any cell can be corrected before export. Supplier names and item names you correct are remembered by GSTIN.   Scanned PDFs: read with OCR (slower, verify amounts).
