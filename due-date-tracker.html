Privacy first

Your deadlines and client notes stay in your browser.

Your profile, completed filings, ARN numbers, checklists and reminders are saved in this browser's local storage, on this device. This page does not send them to a TallySync server.

What does leave your device: Google Fonts load when the page opens. Share on WhatsApp and Official portal links open other sites only when you click them. Calendar, CSV and backup files are created on your device.
