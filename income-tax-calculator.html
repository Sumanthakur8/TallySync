Income Tax Calculator — AY 2026–27

Estimate tax under the new and old regimes for an individual. The new regime slabs are ₹0–4L nil, 4–8L 5%, 8–12L 10%, 12–16L 15%, 16–20L 20%, 20–24L 25%, and above 24L 30%.

New-regime 87A rebate up to ₹60,000 applies where total income is up to ₹12 lakh, subject to applicable rules. This calculator is an estimate and does not model every special-rate income, surcharge, cess nuance, marginal relief, or deduction.
